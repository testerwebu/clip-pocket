## FAQ ideas

### What does ClipPocket do?

ClipPocket keeps your clipboard history in one clean local pocket, so you can find copied text, links, emails, colors and code later.

### Do my clips leave my Mac?

No. ClipPocket is local-first. Your clips stay on your Mac.

### Do I need an account?

No. ClipPocket does not require an account.

### Is this a subscription?

No. ClipPocket launches with simple lifetime pricing.

### Does it support images and files?

Not in V0.1. ClipPocket is designed to support copied images and files later, with local previews where possible.

### Can it search text inside images?

Not in V0.1. Local OCR for copied images is planned for a later phase.
